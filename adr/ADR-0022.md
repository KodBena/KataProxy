# ADR-0022: A Failed Check Carries Its Counterexample

*The failure surface is part of the check.*

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Genre:** Tenet (cross-cutting operational engineering discipline). Governs the evidence path from a check's outcome to the authorized consumer who must act on it. Neither a type-system mandate nor a conduct rule; attaches to the engineered check and the surfaces carrying its outcome, independent of author or runner.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Provenance:** An invariant assertion (2026-10-19) announced one violating instance but supplied only its count; a separate script and an extra LLM review were needed to recover the case and facts. This establishes a diagnostic gap, not the cause of the underlying monotonicity violation.
- **Scope:** Every authored or maintained check that can fail or become unevaluable — invariant assertions, preconditions/validation, boundary refusals, test/CI gates, batch and statistical jobs, resource/temporal limits, service error envelopes — in any language or layer, plus changes to a check's diagnostic output or to a maintained wrapper, aggregator, transport, or retention path carrying it. Applies when artifacts are created or amended; no retroactive sweep.

## Context

The specimen's failure surface:

> `INVARIANT VIOLATION: 1 instance(s) where adding shapes moved the optimum the wrong way`

The assertion raised — satisfying [ADR-0002](0002-fail-loudly.md)'s loudness obligation narrowly — but did not identify the instance, show before/after values, or give the signed delta. **Loudness announces a deviation; diagnosability makes the outcome attributable.** The defect is a break in the evidence path between evaluation and investigation, not mere message wording.

The counterexample is the organizing model, not a universal schema: the evidence may be a violating item, a relation between states, a scoped aggregate, an expired budget, or a statement that the check could not be evaluated. The obligation: preserve bounded, truthful evidence attributable to the specific evaluation, supporting the authorized consumer's next justified step without a forensic rerun to recover facts already available. It does not promise root-cause certainty.

## Definitions

- **Failure surface:** The outcome plus its evidence path (message, structured fields, log/trace event, CI report, retained artifact, or authorized reference). Need not fit one message.
- **Attributable evidence:** Identifies the check and evaluation, and supports what was observed — or why evaluation was impossible.
- **Counterexample:** The evidence model demonstrating why the claim failed: an item, pair/tuple, sequence/path, scoped aggregate, measurement over time, or other witness.
- **Diagnostic closure:** Enough evidence for the intended consumer's next justified step without rediscovering facts the check had or could proportionately have retained. Does not mean root cause is known.

## Decision

Adopted as a cross-cutting tenet in five rules. Spine:

> A failed or unevaluable check preserves bounded, truthful evidence attributable to the specific evaluation that produced its outcome. That evidence supports the intended authorized consumer's next justified investigative or corrective step without reconstructing observations the check could safely and proportionately have retained. Evidence may be carried directly or through a usable reference, and its association with the outcome must survive wrapping, aggregation, and transport. Collection, retention, and presentation respect security and resource limits and must not distort or replace the original outcome.

### Rule 1 — Design evidence with the check

Design the evidence path when authoring or amending a check, not after the first expensive failure. Decide what the consumer must know and how they obtain it (directly, or via a report/artifact/authorized path — a reference counts only if the consumer can follow it). Design, as applicable, for: **which claim and check** (stable identifier for durable checks); **which evaluation** (subject/scope, plus material version, configuration, attempt, seed, or window); **what was observed** (values, relation, statistic, missing prerequisite); **where the evidence is**.

A checklist, not a schema. Evidence form follows check shape:

| Check shape | Attributable evidence |
|---|---|
| Pointwise predicate | Offending case or safe locator, with observed values. |
| Relational predicate | The pair/tuple/sequence/path/snapshots violating the claim. |
| Aggregate/statistical threshold | Scoped statistic, threshold, measurement basis, sample/window; individual examples only if they add evidence. |
| Missing prerequisite | What was sought, scope, which prerequisite failed. |
| Resource/temporal limit | Operation context, budget, observed consumption/elapsed time, window. |
| Unevaluable check | Blocker, partial progress, what remains unknown — not a claimed false predicate. |

Distinguish an evaluated-false claim from an unevaluable one; the latter is its own outcome and must not be reported as an observed violation. Keep observations distinct from causal hypotheses. Prefer stable machine-readable fields rendered to human summaries; prefer existing diff/diagnostic capabilities when they fit — no type, helper, or schema is universally required. Retain costly evidence at the decision point; format lazily on the failure path.

*Enforcement: review-led for sufficiency; existing mechanisms and targeted tests may protect concrete parts. No universal schema implied.*

### Rule 2 — A bare summary must not replace attributable evidence

A count or status is sufficient only when the aggregate itself establishes the violation with the scope and measurement basis needed to interpret it (e.g., a latency gate evidenced by scoped `p95`, threshold, window, and basis). When examples are necessary and available, include useful ones or a usable path; for large result sets state how many are shown and how sampled. Prefer stable, useful examples (high-severity, smaller reproducer) when practical. Report completeness honestly: a total may be exact, a lower bound, an estimate, or unknown — `at least 17 violations; showing 5; evaluation stopped after 10,000 records` beats a misleading total. The summary must not conceal what was observed or left unevaluated.

*Enforcement: review-led for sufficiency; explicit counts/bounds/truncation markers mechanizable where proportionate.*

### Rule 3 — Capture and preserve evidence from the evaluation that produced the outcome

Capture observations at detection. Do not reread mutable state or re-execute an observation to reconstruct earlier evidence; a pure, bounded derivation from retained immutable evidence is permitted. A pointer to mutable state is not capture. Evidence must remain attributable to the specific evaluation/attempt/window — via captured values, attempt identity, snapshot, configuration, seed, or timestamp as contextually needed. A rerun may aid investigation but must not silently replace the original observations.

Preservation continues across the path: a maintained boundary that wraps, translates, aggregates, retries, transports, renders, or retains an outcome must preserve or safely reference the material evidence (a wrapper replacing a rich exception with `operation failed`, a batch summary dropping record locators, or an unretained CI artifact all break this). Retain outcome–evidence association without copying full payloads at every layer. A correlation/trace ID is useful only if it leads to the evidence; it is not itself the evidence.

*Enforcement: review-led for relevance/fidelity; targeted tests can protect capture-at-detection and boundary preservation. No tracing system or new abstraction implied.*

### Rule 4 — Keep evidence collection, retention, and presentation bounded, safe, and honest

The evidence path must not become a source of damage, leakage, or ambiguity. Bound diagnostic **work** (collection, traversal, retention, formatting, emission), not just displayed output.

- **Respect audience and authorization.** Public, operator, and restricted surfaces may carry different detail; use protected paths with safe associations. A "safe identifier" may still reveal existence or enable correlation.
- **Sanitize untrusted material.** No raw rejected input where injection, oversized payloads, or personal data could harm. Redact deliberately; where redaction limits attribution, say so via a safe channel.
- **Do not mutate the subject to explain it.**
- **Do not mask the primary outcome.** A formatting/collection failure must not replace the triggering outcome; expose it separately.
- **Separate observation from hypothesis.** Label suspected causes as hypotheses.
- **Represent limits truthfully.** Exact vs. lower bound vs. estimate vs. unknown; unevaluable ≠ failed.

Priority on conflict: safety/privacy/authorization first; preserve the original outcome unmasked; retain minimum attributing evidence; add supporting values/context; add labeled hypotheses only when useful. If a higher priority blocks a detail, use a safe alternative and state the limitation. Preserve essential values cheaply at detection; defer expensive formatting to failure when safe. Bounded reporting is not license to discard the only attributing evidence; choose an evidence budget and describe its limits honestly.

*Enforcement: review-led for sufficiency/disclosure/non-interference; size, schema, retention, and regression constraints mechanizable where risk and recurrence justify.*

### Rule 5 — Test the diagnostic contract, not only the fact of failure

For durable checks, the material evidence selected under Rules 1–4 is part of the diagnostic contract; where feasible and proportionate, negative-path tests must protect it. Asserting only that a check raises or exits non-zero protects loudness, not diagnosability. The test should fail if the check still reports failure but loses or distorts attributing evidence. Useful tests may verify: the identified case actually failed; reported values belong to the producing evaluation; the threshold/window/context is the one actually used; unevaluable is not reported as violated; wrappers/aggregators preserve material evidence; truncation/bounds/redaction neither imply false completeness nor leak protected data.

Assert stable semantic fields rather than freezing incidental prose; test exact text only when the text is the interface contract. Sufficiency remains review-led. Target non-trivial, high-assurance, or recurring checks; one-off probes need the durable contract only once relied upon. Declare unmechanized surfaces honestly under [ADR-0011 Rule 1](0011-mechanization-discipline.md).

*Enforcement: test/CI gate for concrete contracts where feasible; review-led for sufficiency and exploratory probes. A change that leaves a check failing but drops material contracted evidence must fail the diagnostic-contract test.*

## Worked examples (illustrative, not schemas)

**1. Pointwise numerical invariant** — the specimen, made attributable:

```text
INVARIANT VIOLATION [opt.monotone_under_add]
1 violation found in 240 evaluated instances
case=case_0173  fixture=data/opt/0173.json  seed=8842
shapes: 6 -> 7
optimum: 41.20 -> 43.75  expected: <= 41.20  delta: +2.55
```

*Values illustrative, not facts from the incident.* Stable name, identified evaluation and case, predicate-relevant observations; causal hypothesis deliberately omitted. Expose fixture/seed/IDs only to authorized audiences.

**2. Aggregate threshold:** a latency gate reporting scoped `p95`, threshold, window, sample count, and measurement basis — the statistic *is* the counterexample; on early stop, state the stopping point and total's status rather than imply completeness.

**3. Security-sensitive refusal:** public response stays generic with a reference (`Request could not be completed. Reference: req_7F3A...`); an authorized operator surface resolves it to sanitized evidence. The reference works only if it resolves through an authorized path and is retained long enough; sanitize and bound echoed input everywhere.

## Anti-pattern checklist

| Pattern | Why incomplete |
|---|---|
| Count with no attributable evidence when the violating case is available and needed | Doesn't establish which evaluated claim failed. |
| Aggregate without scope/threshold/window/basis | Uninterpretable number. |
| Negative-path test checking only `raises`/non-zero exit | Diagnostic fidelity can regress silently. |
| Rerun used only to recover facts available at detection | Discards evidence; forces rediscovery. |
| Wrapper/aggregator replacing or detaching material evidence | Breaks outcome–justification path. |
| Short report via unbounded collection/formatting work | Unbounded cost/DoS risk. |
| Formatter failure replacing the original outcome | Hides the reported event. |
| Raw untrusted input echoed to a diagnostic surface | Injection/disclosure/exhaustion hazards. |
| Suspected cause stated as observed | Conflates evidence and hypothesis. |
| Partial evaluation reported as exact total | Claims unobtained knowledge. |

## Enforcement surface (ADR-0011)

No repository-wide schema, lint, helper, or automatic gate is claimed to exist. Evidence-sufficiency judgment is **review-led** at filing; durable checks' concrete contracts should get targeted tests where proportionate, preferring existing mechanisms. Declare enforcement per [ADR-0011 Rule 1](0011-mechanization-discipline.md). On recurrence, [ADR-0011 Rule 2](0011-mechanization-discipline.md) governs conversion to the **smallest effective mechanism** (regression test, schema field, envelope contract, helper, output bound, retention assertion, lint, checklist item). Recurrence does not automatically justify a universal schema. Claimed enforcement must describe what is implemented, not intended.

## What this tenet does not mean

- **Not a universal schema** — evidence depends on claim, evaluation, audience, next step.
- **Not a root-cause oracle** — prevents avoidable rediscovery; causal investigation may remain.
- **Not a dump-everything/observability mandate** — bounded by security, cost, operations.
- **Not a requirement to expose counterexamples publicly** — surfaces differ in disclosure obligations.
- **Not a type-system mandate** — structural prevention ([ADR-0000](0000-the-alpha-and-the-omega-type-driven-design.md), [ADR-0012](0012-compositional-and-structural-hygiene.md)) is neither replaced nor required.
- **Not a conduct rule** — [ADR-0013](0013-execution-integrity.md) governs actor integrity; this governs the artifact's evidence path.
- **Not witness-construction doctrine** — [ADR-0021](0021-witness-construction-discipline.md) governs witness construction; this governs the outcome's evidence path.
- **Not a rule for passing-check coverage** — vacuous-pass detection is a separate concern.
- **Not a retroactive sweep** — applies to new and amended checks and paths.

## Alternatives considered

- **A: Fold into ADR-0002.** Rejected: loudness ≠ evidence-path fidelity; a check can keep raising while losing material evidence — a testable, distinct violation.
- **B: Fold into ADR-0012 P5.** Rejected: P5 concerns root-cause removal vs. band-aids; this governs outcome evidence, composing with but not subsumed by P5.
- **C: Mandate one universal error schema/helper.** Rejected: checks span languages/layers/audiences; a fixed schema over- or under-fits. A shared mechanism may later be the smallest effective fix for a demonstrated class.
- **D: Treat as message style/reviewer preference.** Rejected: the specimen required separate recovery effort; diagnosability is a testable engineering property.

**ADR-0008 classification rationale:** the distinct object is the **evidence path tied to a check outcome** — attribution, fidelity, safe availability, boundary preservation — not ADR-0002's loudness, ADR-0012's structural hygiene, or ADR-0021's witness construction. Revisit placement if the distinction fails in practice.

## Consequences

**Positive:** outcomes attributable without forensic reruns; evidence shaped to the claim (not forced into item counts); evidence survives wrappers/retries/aggregation/transport; authorized consumers served without public over-disclosure; negative-path tests protect evidence fidelity, not just failure.

**Negative:** some checks need extra capture, structure, retention, or tests; sufficiency still requires judgment; security/resource limits may force partial evidence (represented honestly); a complete evidence path cannot guarantee a short investigation.

## Revisit when…

1. A diagnostic gap recurs after recording ([ADR-0011 Rule 2](0011-mechanization-discipline.md)): convert to smallest effective mechanism, name its surface.
2. A recurring check class cannot preserve attribution through its boundaries: pick the right smallest remedy.
3. A shared diagnostic schema/helper is proposed: evaluate against demonstrated omission classes, not uniformity.
4. A check cannot safely provide expected evidence: record the limit and safe alternative, not a broad exception.
5. Boundaries with adjacent tenets blur: tighten this ADR's scope rather than broadening into conduct, witness construction, or general observability.
